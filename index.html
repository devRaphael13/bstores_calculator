📋 Business Rules

  • No credit transactions. All transactions must be completed with immediate payment, payment validate order
  • Total cost first. Every sale must cover: Cost Price + Waybill + Transport.
  • Minimum selling price. Your break-even point, sell at or above this to avoid a loss.
  • Suggested price. Adds your chosen profit margin on top of break-even cost.
  • Confirm waybill rates. Logistics prices change — check with your rider/dispatch before quoting.
  • No Free delivery. Only offer free delivery when you're sure you'd have profit afterwards.
